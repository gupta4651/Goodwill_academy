## ❌ AVOID AT ALL COSTS

- **Link buying services** (Fiverr, cheap SEO services)
- **Private Blog Networks (PBNs)** - Google bans these
- **Comment spam** - Auto-commenting on blogs
- **Link wheels** - Linking sites in circles
- **Automated link building software**

These result in Google penalties: `-100` rank drop or complete deindexing.
